Methodology and risk

A filing is evidence, not a forecast.

  • Direct, non-derivative common-stock open-market purchases coded P must total at least $50,000.
  • Direct open-market sales coded S must total at least $250,000.
  • Grants, awards, option exercises, tax withholding, gifts, derivatives, indirect holdings, and ambiguous transactions stay outside the ranking.
  • Position change estimates use reported transaction shares and direct shares held afterward. They are not percentages of an insider’s total personal portfolio.
  • This edition covers SEC corporate-insider filings only. It does not include congressional trading disclosures.
  • Public-filing research only. This is not financial advice, and past outcomes do not predict future returns.
